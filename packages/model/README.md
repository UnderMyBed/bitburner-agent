# @bitburner-agent/model

The fast model ([#13](https://github.com/UnderMyBed/bitburner-agent/issues/13), [architecture §4.3](../../docs/architecture.md#43-fast-model)): a pure, deterministic model of the game for the planner ([#14](https://github.com/UnderMyBed/bitburner-agent/issues/14)) to roll candidate policies forward on, far faster than the real-engine simulator (2–3× real time once the batcher fills the cloud servers, [#247](https://github.com/UnderMyBed/bitburner-agent/issues/247)). Pure like core (no Node, no DOM), and depending on core only: `core` ← `model` ← the planner; the brain's tests import it, to check it against the brain.

> **Status:** slices 1 to 3 ([#260](https://github.com/UnderMyBed/bitburner-agent/issues/260), [#263](https://github.com/UnderMyBed/bitburner-agent/issues/263), [#294](https://github.com/UnderMyBed/bitburner-agent/issues/294)'s items 1, 2, 3 and 5): BN1's cycles of reputation, favour and augmentations; the money in hand by the brain's own purchases, one a medium snapshot, cloud servers by its own income measure; the joins in the time the brain and the game take, the backdoors one at a time and the joins one a slow snapshot; the share by #242's rule, the batches' use from the state; A6's countdown, holds and lookahead; the paired comparison, a verdict a start, over the joins' time, the first cloud server's, a wider `firstCycleSpeed` and the count's miss: no answer on the donations from any start; three answers (count first sooner) that lean on the tail, for the record, not #247's. Fitted on run #3 alone (refitted in #294), but for the curves' tail past 120 min, fitted on BN1.3 (#310), which is **seen, no longer held out**: #294 was planned from its figures. **Not fit for #247 yet** ([the verdict](#the-verdict-not-fit-for-247-yet)): on BN1.3's own curves the rules follow its whole run, and the fitted curves now catch its 5th cycle's quick install and phases 1 and 2, but miss phase 3 (and BN1.3's every Daedalus cycle ran out of their range before #310's tail), and the money after a cycle's first cloud server runs away. **Held out on BN1.4 ([#310](https://github.com/UnderMyBed/bitburner-agent/issues/310)), cycles 2 to 14, it misses #263's bar** ([the verdict](#the-verdict-not-fit-for-247-yet), item 7): the curves' tail past 120 min, fitted on BN1.3, holds on BN1.4's Daedalus cycle, but from its 8th cycle the level runs 31% to 61% high, the money after the first cloud server runs away, the model makes 1 of BN1.4's 6 quick installs (5 in cycles 3 to 7, the 11th), and the share at hacking 1.9k to 2.7k gives the cloud away where BN1.4's batches held it. BN1.3's Daedalus cycles are in range now, but only by the tail fitted on them. Not wired into the brain's choices ([owner decision 46](../../docs/decisions/owner-decisions.md)).

## Why a package of its own

Core is the game's facts and formulas, imported by the in-game code too; the model adds what isn't the game's: curves measured from a run. The brain is a process with a store; the model is a library the planner and the studies call. So it lives between them, and the brain's choices it makes are core's, moved there from the brain in #260 (A5's work, A6's reach and decision, the city group, home RAM's price), or core's own (#242's share, `shareRound`; #294's batch plan): what it runs is what the brain runs. The glue and the constants it still copies are tested against the brain on the same inputs ("brain against the fast model"):

- in `packages/brain/test/augmentations.test.ts` (its rule's `installTiming` "count rule": since #343 the brain's planner times installs short of Daedalus's count, and the model's policies stay the count rule's, the brains it was fitted on), the countdown's start, extensions, end and call-off; the countdown's and the lookahead's reserves; #243's hold, its window and `assumeMoney` after a restart; and the constants it copies, checked equal (the share's home RAM kept and in use among them: the brain's reserved constants, not the game's used RAM);
- in `packages/brain/test/desired-state.test.ts`, the purchase chosen (a program, home RAM or a cloud server) from the same purse, money and income, over an empty cloud, a program or home's RAM first, the next one held back, money short and a payback not met; and what the countdown, Daedalus's $100b and The Red Pill's donation hold back from it;
- in `packages/brain/test/cloud-servers.test.ts` (#294), the income measure the cloud purchases pay back on: the model's `IncomeWindow` gives the brain's `HackLoopIncome` rate, sample by sample, over 25 minutes of medium snapshots with purchases and growing RAM;
- in `packages/brain/test/backdoors.test.ts` (#294), a hacker faction's join: the brain's own `BackdoorRule` on the Agent's medium snapshots, one server at a time, and its `chooseJoin` picking which invitation to join at a slow snapshot, with the Agent's rounds and tries, the game's backdoor and its invitation check on a clock of its own between; the mean over the clocks' phases within 0.3 s of the model's `JoinPipeline`, for BitRunners alone, an 8.5-s and a 9.5-s backdoor that miss the next medium snapshot (the first by the Agent's round), one at the edge (107.8 s), two over the tries' 30 s, and all four due at once; and the constants the model copies (the Agent's tries, the game's check, the gate's snapshots) read from their sources.

## The cycle model

```ts
import {
  comparisonByStart,
  FAVOUR_FIRST_POLICY,
  pairedComparison,
  RUN_3_CYCLES,
  RUN_3_POLICY,
  runModel,
  TODAYS_POLICY,
} from "@bitburner-agent/model";

const run = runModel({ currentNode: 1, ownedSF: { "1": 1 } }, { policy: TODAYS_POLICY });
run.cycles;        // each install: when, why, what, Daedalus's count, hacking, money, reputation, favour, share
run.events;        // joins, installs, donations unlocked, the destroy
run.destroyedAtMs; // or null within the horizon (24 h)
run.outOfRange;    // the cycles extrapolated past the curves' bound: a run with any isn't to be trusted
// On run #3's own measured curves rather than the fitted one:
runModel({ currentNode: 1, ownedSF: { "1": 1 } }, { policy: RUN_3_POLICY, measured: RUN_3_CYCLES });
// From an install within a BitNode (#310): what's installed, each faction's favour, home's RAM.
runModel({ currentNode: 1, ownedSF: { "1": 3 }, afterInstall: { installed, favor, homeRamGb } });
// The structural variants: A6 seeing the gross, the share by install index (#263); the joins at once, or later (#294).
runModel({ currentNode: 1, ownedSF: { "1": 1 } }, { money: "gross", share: "index", joinDelay: "instant" });
// #247's paired comparison: two policies at each setting of a grid, a difference only where every setting agrees.
pairedComparison({ currentNode: 1, ownedSF: { "1": 1 } }, TODAYS_POLICY, FAVOUR_FIRST_POLICY).verdict;
// And a verdict for each of `COMPARISON_STARTS`, crossed with the whole grid (#294); a start under 32 GB gives none.
comparisonByStart(TODAYS_POLICY, FAVOUR_FIRST_POLICY);
```

`runModel(start, { policy, calibration, measured, money, share, joinDelay, cloudDelayMs, horizonMs, stepMs, onStep, onWork })` steps a BitNode a minute at a time from its start (or from an install within it, `ModelStart.afterInstall`, #310) to its destroy, about 0.25 s a run locally. Each step:

- **The level and the gross income** come from the fitted curve (`Curves`, below; the money's clock at most `moneyPaceMost` times the reference's till the cycle's first cloud server) at the multipliers installed (core's `installGains`, NeuroFlux Governor's levels each, and SF1's bonus), or from a measured cycle (`measured`, `measuredAt`). Within the step, the clocks run a medium snapshot (10 s) at a time.
- **The money in hand** (`money.ts`, #263): the gross less what the brain spends through the cycle, **one purchase a medium snapshot** (#294: the brain chooses the next from the snapshot that shows the last done), by its own rules (`nextPurchase`; `desired-state.ts` `#choosePurchase`, `cloud-servers.ts`):
  - a program or home's RAM, the cheaper of those the money covers: a program not in the countdown nor into The Red Pill's donation's money; home's RAM not into any money A6 holds;
  - else a cloud server, once the brain's income measure has a full window: **the money's rises between medium snapshots over the hack loop's hosts' RAM × time, across the last 10 minutes** (`IncomeWindow`, the brain's `HackLoopIncome`, #207), from the Agent's first usable medium snapshot after an install (`FIRST_INCOME_SAMPLE_MS`, 20 s), so the first server comes at 10 min 20 s at the soonest; the largest power of two the money covers, less what A6 holds from cloud servers and the next program or home's RAM if the income brings it within a minute, paid back within 15 min at that income a GB. A purchase between two snapshots hides that stretch's rise, and a server just bought weighs only from its first snapshot, as in the brain.

  The step's own purchase comes after A6's decision, under the holds it has just set (the brain's one update). Programs and cloud servers are lost at an install; home's RAM is kept, and the commit buys more with the money above the set's, the donations' and the levels' (`homeRamAtCommit`). With `money: "gross"`, A6 and the joins see the gross instead (#260's money); the purse still spends through the cycle, and the commit's home RAM is from the cash left, so nothing is counted twice.
- **The joins** (`joins.ts`, #294): a hacker faction once the level reaches its server's (`HACKER_FACTION_JOINS`) and its port openers are bought; Tian Di Hui at hacking 50 and $1m; the city group by core's `cityGroupValues` and `chooseCityGroup` (owner decision 54) once the money covers its invitations; Daedalus with its count, hacking 2500 and $100b. **Each joins as long after its terms as the brain and the game take**, the mean over the clocks' phases (`JoinPipeline`): the Agent's rounds carry its medium and slow snapshots, counted from the same round, and the game's invitation check runs on its own. For a hacker faction, the medium snapshot after the terms (the brain's `chooseBackdoor`), the Agent's round, the backdoor's quarter hack time at security 1 (`Terminal/Terminal.ts:242`), the game's check every 2 s (`engine.tsx:177-182`), the slow snapshot after the invitation (the dialog answered, the Factions page read), the join in the Agent's round, and the slow snapshot after that, which A5 works from: 100 s where the backdoor ends well before the next medium snapshot, 110 s where it ends after, and between where the check's 2 s straddle it (107.8 s for a 7.6-s backdoor). The join's own round and a city faction's gate fall inside the minute to the next slow snapshot, so they never move a join's time: no parity case can see them, by design. The backdoors go one at a time (the next asked for at the medium snapshot after the last's reported done, 40 s), and the joins one a slow snapshot, in the order invited, whatever their kind: all four hacker factions due at once, BitRunners joins 280 s after its terms. Tian Di Hui a travel in place of the backdoor; a city group a city a slow snapshot, each join through the irreversible gate within its minute; Daedalus the game's check and the slow snapshots. The terms were met within the step that shows them, half a step back on average, and the faction is joined at the step nearest the slow snapshot that shows it. `joinDelay: "instant"` joins at the step the terms are met (slice 2's), a number that many ms later than modelled.
- **A5's work:** core's `factionWorkChoice`, at core's `factionReputationPerSecond` with the level, `faction_rep`, the favour and the share bonus, focused (owner decision 37). The share bonus is the game's formula on the share threads: by #242's rule for today's brain (`CyclePolicy.homeShares`, absent or true; `shareRamGbOfState`), core's `shareRound` on home, less the Agent's 8 GB in use (`HOME_USED_BY_THE_AGENT_GB`) and the 32 it keeps (`SHARE_HOME_KEPT_GB`, both the brain's), and on the batches' hosts, which share only up to 80% use, at **their use from the state** (#294, `batches.ts`: core's batch plan at its most on the target worth most among those the level and the ports reach, `batchesPeakGbOf`, over the hosts' RAM: the hosts fill in the early cycles, and at a high level the target's money bounds the batches, so most of the cloud is left to the share); for run #3's brain and the simulator probe's, before #242, a fitted share (`shareRamShare`) of home's and the cloud servers' RAM; or with `share: "index"` run #3's own by install index (#260's). Every other joined faction gains passive reputation (core's `passiveFactionReputationPerSecond`), special factions aside.
- **A6:** core's `augmentationShops`, `augmentationEndgame` (A8's The Red Pill, "Daedalus donations" and the holds) and `installJudgement` on the set the money covers (`installDecision`), with the lookahead's reserve at the income over the brain's 10-minute window; #243's hold (`countHoldOf`, the money taken as coming while its window is open and there's no income, as the brain's `assumeMoney`); the countdown (`installClock`: 90 s, 30 s for a quick install, extended as the count rises, called off when the rule stops); what the purchases leave alone after it (`holdsOf`). A step is a minute, so the install comes at the first step at or past the countdown's end.
- **An install** banks each faction's favour (`favorAfterInstall`), installs the set and the levels, and starts the next cycle with nothing joined, a fresh income measure and no join under way.
- **The destroy:** The Red Pill installed and the level at w0r1d_d43m0n's 3000.

What it leaves out: the hack loop's root (every 30 s; the level, not the port opener, comes last in the model's runs); a slow snapshot that waits a round or more for time to read the factions; the batches' ramp on a cycle's new RAM (the money after the first cloud server, [the verdict](#the-verdict-not-fit-for-247-yet)); the batcher's preparation of its target and the next one prepared beside the batches; the time the commit takes (the brain commits one intent an update: the donations, each augmentation, home's RAM, then NeuroFlux Governor a level at a time, `augmentations.ts` `#neuroFlux`); the money a cycle may start with; combat, charisma and companies; the gang, Bladeburners and every BitNode's curves but BN1's. (`spendThroughCycle`, #263's every purchase the money covers in a step, is gone: `nextPurchase` and `afterPurchase` make one purchase a medium snapshot.)

**Extrapolation, flagged and bounded (#263, #310):** past its last reading (120 min) each curve runs on at its last slope, and a measured cycle past its end likewise. The curves' range is that reading and the tail checked past it on a live cycle (`Calibration.tailCheckedMin`, #310: 140 min on the experience's clock, 120 on the money's, fitted on BN1.3 and checked on BN1.4; `Curves.range` 260 and 240). Each cycle says how many minutes it ran past its range (`extrapolatedMinutes`, the clock furthest past); one more than `EXTRAPOLATION_BOUND_MIN` (60) past its range is out of range (so past 320 and 300 min), and the run lists it (`outOfRange`), the cycle under way at the horizon included.

### Policies

A `CyclePolicy` is what the planner varies (architecture §4.5.3): `daedalusCountHold` (#243), `favourGrind(view)` (a joined faction to work for toward the favour that unlocks its donations, in place of A5's choice; one not joined, or with that reputation earned, leaves A5's), and `install(view, brainsReason)` (whether to install now; never asked about The Red Pill or an endgame hold, never installs nothing). `homeShares` is #242's share, a brain's version rather than a planner's choice (absent: today's). `TODAYS_POLICY` is the brain on main; `RUN_3_POLICY` is run #3's brain (main 7c741cc), before #243's hold and #242's share; `FAVOUR_FIRST_POLICY` is #247's "favour first" (`src/compare.ts`).

## The validation

From figures the issues state (`test/fixtures.ts`), never a store. Each check is labelled; the tolerance is in brackets.

- **Independent:** a prediction, nothing of the run checked fitted on it.
- **Downstream:** the rules on a run's own measured level and income a cycle. They share inputs with what they check, so they test the mechanism, not the curves.
- **Fitted:** a check on the run the curves were fitted on, of what they were fitted to: consistency only. Since #310 this includes BN1.3's Daedalus cycles, in range only by the tail fitted on them.
- **Held out:** BN1.4's cycles 2 to 14 (#310), the curves' tail included: it's fitted on BN1.3 (`tailCheckedMin`).
- **The game:** the model's formulas (core's `installGains` and SF1's bonus) against the multipliers the game showed.
- **The share (state):** core's batch plan and #242's rule on the game's own level, multipliers and RAM: a check of item 5's rule, not the path.
- **The curves:** the fitted curves run on a cycle's own multipliers, home RAM and first cloud server (`replayCycle`), with no rule moving them: a check of the curves, not the path.
- **Seen (no longer held out):** BN1.3. #263's part 2 held it out; its first review then cited its figures, and #294 was planned from them, so it can't validate any more. The curves are fitted on run #3 alone (#294's refit), and #294's mechanisms are the brain's code and the game's, tested against them; but #310 fits the curves' tail past 120 min on BN1.3 (`tailCheckedMin`), so a check that leans on the tail is fitted. BN1.4 (#310) was the held-out check; the next live run checks the batches' ramp.
- **Out of range:** the check's cycle, or one before it, ran past the curves' range (`ModelRun.outOfRange`): no validation either way, whatever its figure. Every check asserts its run's range. The bound (60 min past the curves' range, `Curves.range`: the last reading and the tail checked past it) was set for the fitted curves; on measured curves a cycle in range may still run some minutes past its own end, which the BN1.3 table states.
