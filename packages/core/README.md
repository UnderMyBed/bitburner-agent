# @bitburner-agent/core

Shared model types, interfaces and game facts, imported by the brain, the in-game code and the simulator alike, so it has neither Node nor DOM types ([CONTRIBUTING](../../CONTRIBUTING.md#typescript-configs)). Everything is exported from `src/index.ts`. Game facts are checked against the pinned game (`vendor/bitburner-src`): names and constants against its source (`test/`), and the generated data below against its engine.

## Augmentations and factions

Built in #193 for the brain's faction and augmentation choices (M4 phase A, slices A5, A6 and A8).

### The data

Generated from the pinned game by the simulator's engine, never typed by hand: `node packages/sim/scripts/generate-game-data.ts` writes it ([sim: Generated game data](../sim/README.md#generated-game-data)), and `packages/sim/test/game-data.test.ts` fails if any entry differs from the engine's. After a game bump, rerun the generator.

- **`AUGMENTATIONS`** (`src/augmentation-data.ts`): every augmentation by name, in the game's order. Each has its base money cost and base reputation (`Infinity` in both for BigD's Big ... Brain, and in reputation for violet Congruity Implant and Z.O.Ë.; no faction sells those three), the factions that sell it, its prerequisites, its multipliers other than 1 (by the game's names: `hacking`, `faction_rep`, `hacking_money`, …), the game's `isSpecial`, and what an install gives (`startingMoney`, `programs`). `isSpecial` keeps an augmentation out of a gang's shop, grafting and sleeves, not out of a faction's own shop: NeuroFlux Governor is special. Unstable Circadian Modulator's multipliers are drawn by the game's clock when the game loads and at each install and BitNode reset, so it has none and `randomMultipliers: true`.
- **`AUGMENTATION_BITNODE_MULTIPLIERS`** (same file): each BitNode's `AugmentationMoneyCost`, `AugmentationRepCost` and `DaedalusAugsRequirement`, and since #203 `FactionWorkRepGain` (faction work's reputation), at level 1.
- **`FACTIONS`** (`src/faction-data.ts`): every faction by name, with its enemies, the work it offers, whether membership survives an install, and its invitation requirements in the form `ns.singularity.getFactionInviteRequirements` gives (`PlayerRequirement`), as in BN1 with nothing backdoored. Daedalus's augmentation count is the BitNode's (`daedalusAugmentationsRequired`), and a megacorp's company reputation is 3/4 once its server is backdoored.

### The rules

`src/augmentations.ts`, each the game's own (cited there), and equal to the engine's to the last bit for every augmentation in the parity suite's states:

| Function | What |
|---|---|
| `augmentationCost(name, state)` | Its price now: `{ money, reputation }`, the game's `getAugCost`. Money grows 1.9× (less SF11's discount) for each augmentation queued, Shadows of Anarchy's excepted; then the BitNode's multipliers. NeuroFlux Governor's money and reputation are also × 1.14 per level, installed and queued. Shadows of Anarchy's are × 7 and × 1.3 per one of theirs owned, with no BitNode multiplier. |
| `purchaseSequenceCost(names, state)` | Buying `names` in that order: each one's price at its turn, and the money in all. It throws on one the game wouldn't sell then: owned already, sold by no faction, or a prerequisite missing. |
| `missingPrerequisites(name, state)` | Its prerequisites neither installed nor queued. |
| `augmentationsSoldBy(faction, bitNode, gangFaction)` | A faction's shop, without The Red Pill at Daedalus in BN15 (a darknet reward there). The gang's faction's (`gangFaction`, #334, `FactionHelpers.tsx:172-202`): every augmentation not special nor the violet Congruity Implant (so no NeuroFlux Governor), The Red Pill too in BN2; exact in BN1 and BN2, where `GangUniqueAugs` is 1 and the game's seeded draw keeps every faction-unique one; elsewhere what no draw drops (the shared ones and the faction's own), cautious. Pass null with no gang. |
| `augmentationBitNodeMultipliers(currentNode, ownedSF)` | The BitNode's multipliers on augmentations, from `resetInfo`, BN12's at its level. |
| `daedalusAugmentationsRequired(currentNode, ownedSF)` | The installed augmentations Daedalus's invitation needs. |

`state` is what the brain has: `installed` is `resetInfo.ownedAugs`; `queued` is the queued augmentations in order, one entry per NeuroFlux Governor level; `sourceFile11Level` is `resetInfo.ownedSF["11"] ?? 0` (the game has already applied the BitNode options' overrides); and `bitNodeMultipliers` is `augmentationBitNodeMultipliers(resetInfo.currentNode, resetInfo.ownedSF)`. An unknown augmentation, an installed level that isn't a whole number from 1, an SF11 level outside 0-3, or a multiplier that isn't finite and above 0 throws.

### Faction work

`src/faction-work.ts` ([#203](https://github.com/UnderMyBed/bitburner-agent/issues/203)), for the brain's choice of which faction to work for, and how soon its next augmentation comes ([packages/brain](../brain/README.md#factions-and-their-work)):

| Function | What |
|---|---|
| `factionReputationPerCycle(person, workType, favor, sourceFile15Level, factionWorkRepGain, shareBonus?)` | The reputation a cycle of hacking, field or security work earns, focused: the game's `calculateFactionRep` (`Work/Formulas.ts:82-89`, `PersonObjects/formulas/reputation.ts`). Hacking: (hacking + intelligence/3) / 975; field: 0.9 × (the five combat-and-charisma skills + hacking + intelligence) / 975 / 5.5; security: the same without charisma, / 4.5; each × `faction_rep` × the intelligence bonus × (1 + favour/100) × the BitNode's `FactionWorkRepGain`, and SF15.3's charisma terms. A figure that isn't finite throws. |
| `factionReputationPerSecond(person, workType, { favor, sourceFile15Level, focused, implant, factionWorkRepGain, shareBonus? })` | Five cycles a second (`MilliPerCycle`, 200 ms), × 0.8 unfocused unless the Neuroreceptor Management Implant is installed (`focusPenalty`). |
| `hackingValue(augmentation, hackingLevel)` | What it's worth to the brain's hacking at the player's hacking level (#203 review; A6 ranks purchases by it too): the natural logarithms of its `HACKING_MULTIPLIERS` (speed, money, growth, chance, experience, faction reputation) added up, and its `hacking` skill multiplier as `hackingSkillValue` at that level; 0 for one with none. `hacking_speed` counts once, though it brings money and experience sooner. |
| `hackingSkillValue(multiplier, hackingLevel)` | ×m on the hacking skill, as the experience multiplier that lifts the level as much: ln x = (L/32)(1 − 1/m), from the level formula m × (32 ln(exp + 534.6) − 200) (`Person.ts:60-63`, `formulas/skill.ts:13`). At level 300 BitWire's ×1.05 is worth ×1.56 on experience, at 3000 ×87: summing logarithms alone would undervalue it 9 times over at 300. |

`person` is `ns.getPlayer()`'s seven skills and its `faction_rep` multiplier (`WorkingPerson`); the intelligence bonus is `intelligenceBonus`, the hacking ceilings' (below). The share bonus (#224) is an argument, 1 by default: it multiplies all of hacking work's rate, and only the (hacking + intelligence, plus SF15.3's charisma for field) term of field and security work's (`reputation.ts:16-52`; [sharing spare RAM](#sharing-spare-ram)). The BitNode's `FactionWorkRepGain` is the generated table's (`AUGMENTATION_BITNODE_MULTIPLIERS`, 0.2 in BN14, 0.5 in BN2; `augmentationBitNodeMultipliers`), checked against the engine. `test/faction-work.test.ts` works each by hand and checks the constants and terms against the game's source; the brain's `faction-work-parity.test.ts` checks the rate against the engine's `ns.formulas.work.factionGains` for every work type, over people and favours that differ in every figure it reads.

## Favour and donations

`src/donations.ts` (#225, owner decision 53): a faction's favour, banked at each install, and the reputation money buys once the favour unlocks donations, from the game's formulas (`Faction/formulas/favor.ts`, `donation.ts`), each checked against `ns.formulas.reputation` in the brain's parity suite (`packages/brain/test/donation-parity.test.ts`).

| Function | What |
|---|---|
| `favorToRep(favor)`, `repToFavor(reputation)` | Favour's worth in reputation, `25000 × (1.02^f − 1)` with the game's own ln 1.02, and back, never above `MAX_FAVOR` (35,331). |
| `favorAfterInstall(favor, reputation)` | The favour after the next install with that reputation earned this cycle (`addRepToFavor`). |
| `favorToDonate(multipliers)`, `donationsUnlocked(favor, multipliers)` | The favour that unlocks donations, `floor(150 × FavorToDonateToFaction)`: 150 in BN1, 75 in BN3, 0 in BN8, 153 in BN12.1; and whether a favour has it. `FavorToDonateToFaction` is in `AUGMENTATION_BITNODE_MULTIPLIERS`, generated from the engine. |
| `reputationToUnlockDonations(favor, multipliers)` | The least whole reputation this cycle that makes the next install's favour the threshold: 462,491 from none in BN1; 0 once unlocked. |
| `repFromDonation(amount, factionRep, factionWorkRepGain)`, `donationForReputation(...)` | A donation's reputation, `amount / 1e6 × faction_rep × FactionWorkRepGain` (favour doesn't multiply it), and back. |
| `donationToReach(reputation, factionRep, factionWorkRepGain)` | The least whole dollars whose reputation is that and `DONATION_REPUTATION_MARGIN` (0.01) more, so the faction's reputation reaches a price despite the floating point of adding it. What the brain plans and donates. |
| `acceptsDonations(faction, gangFaction)` | Whether a joined faction takes donations at all: not the player's gang's, and one that offers work (not Bladeburners, Church of the Machine God or Shadows of Anarchy). |

`src/intents.ts` has `donateToFaction { faction, amount, reputation }`: done once the faction's reputation is `reputation`.

## Augmentations and the reset

`src/factions.ts` also has Daedalus's invitation requirements (#215): `daedalusRequirements` (the BitNode's count of installed augmentations, $100b, hacking 2500 or every combat skill 1500, `Faction/FactionInfo.tsx:138-149`) and `daedalusMissing`, what's still missing, worded; `daedalusSkillsReached` (#243), whether the hacking or combat requirement is met. `InstallReason` has the brain's own "The Red Pill".

And (#228) `TIAN_DI_HUI`, `TIAN_DI_HUI_CITIES` (Chongqing, New Tokyo, Ishima), `TIAN_DI_HUI_HACKING` (50) and `TIAN_DI_HUI_MONEY` ($1m) are Tian Di Hui's invitation (`Faction/FactionInfo.tsx:680-692`).

`src/augmentation-plan.ts` (#210, M4 phase A slice A6): which augmentations the brain buys, in what order, and when it installs them: the legacy scripts' count rule (#222, owner decision 51; `alainbryden/bitburner-scripts`' autopilot.js, each constant citing its line). The functions are pure and use the game's formulas. The brain's rule (`packages/brain/src/augmentations.ts`) feeds them what the snapshots say. An augmentation's worth is A5's `hackingValue` (`src/faction-work.ts`, #203) at the player's hacking level.
